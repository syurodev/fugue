API: New([]KnownValue, Options) *Detector; (*Detector).Find(string) []Finding;
Find(string) []Finding for detection without known values; Mask(string, []Finding) string.
KnownValue carries Value, Kind and optional env Key. Options carries AllowKeys,
AllowPaths and Path. Rebuild by constructing and replacing a detector, retaining no
caller-owned slices. Findings carry only Start, End, Kind and Key, never values.

Use stdlib Aho–Corasick with longest suffix outputs, merging overlapping known spans;
linear byte-indexed candidate selection gives stable priority without sorting matches.
Fixed regexps are read-only after package initialization. Assignment parsing is line-based.
No new module, I/O, session state, placeholders, envelopes or reverse substitution.

Review: partial cross-source overlaps now union under the highest-priority kind;
legacy encrypted PEM headers and CRLF delimiters have regression fixtures.
Targeted race/shuffle tests and architecture checks pass. Full task check passes, including all eight short-fuzz targets.
